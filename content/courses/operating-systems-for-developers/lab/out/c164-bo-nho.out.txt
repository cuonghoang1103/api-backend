# Drill M5 — effective access time with a TLB ||| Bài M5 — thời gian truy cập hiệu dụng có TLB
$ python3 osgcalc.py eat 10 80 0.95
hit:  TLB 10 + memory 80 = 90 ns
miss: TLB 10 + 1 page-table read(s) 80 x 1 + memory 80 = 170 ns
EAT = 0.95 x 90 + 0.05 x 170 = 94 ns (vs 80 ns with no paging)
$ python3 osgcalc.py eat 10 80 0.95 4
hit:  TLB 10 + memory 80 = 90 ns
miss: TLB 10 + 4 page-table read(s) 80 x 4 + memory 80 = 410 ns
EAT = 0.95 x 90 + 0.05 x 410 = 106 ns (vs 80 ns with no paging)
$ python3 osgcalc.py eat 10 80 0.80 4
