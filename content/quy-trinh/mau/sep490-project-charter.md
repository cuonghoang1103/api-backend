# Project Charter

> **Purpose:** one page that authorises the capstone project — why it exists, what is in and out, who decides, the milestones and how success is measured. Signed (or confirmed by e-mail) by the supervisor before Report 1 is due.
> **Who fills:** the leader (hat C1 Leader/PM); reviewed with the supervisor in week 1–2.
> **Guide:** every objective is measurable; every out-of-scope item is written down — the defense panel asks "why didn't you build X?".
