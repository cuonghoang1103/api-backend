Behind the scene

Thanks for stopping by this playground!
It is the 3D front door to cuongthai.com — drive into any gate and you land in that part of the site. Here is what it is built on.

Original project

This world is a fork of folio-2025, the open-source portfolio by Bruno Simon (bruno-simon.com), released under the MIT license. Huge thanks to him for opening up a project of this scale.
The engine, the island and the driving are his work. The identity, the areas, the 3D letters and the vocabulary game are mine.

Three.js

Three.js is the library rendering this 3D world.
It was created by mr.doob (X, GitHub), followed by hundreds of awesome developers, one of which being Sunag (X, GitHub) who added TSL, enabling the use of both WebGL and WebGPU, making this playground possible.

The vocabulary game

Smash a brick wall with the car and an English word pops up. The words come straight from the My Language section of the site.
